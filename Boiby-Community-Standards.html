Community Standards

Boiby is a place for connection and civility. To ensure a safe environment, we enforce the following standards.

Safety & Well-being

Child Safety

We have zero tolerance for any behavior that risks the safety of minors, this includes the following:

  • Predatory Behavior: Any attempt to manipulate, groom, or exploit a minor.
  • Sexualization: Soliciting sexual material or engaging in sexual conversation with minors.
  • Illegal Content: Sharing, requesting, or discussing child exploitation material (CSAM).
Violence & Extremism

We prohibit the promotion of violence or the organization of dangerous activities, this includes the following:

  • Glorification: Supporting or praising extremist attacks, leaders, or organizations.
  • Recruitment: Encouraging others to join extremist groups or fundraising for them.
  • Violent Threats: Threatening real-world harm, mass shootings, or domestic terrorism.
Self-Harm & Harmful Behavior

We prohibit content that encourages or provides instruction for self-inflicted harm, this includes the following:

  • Promotion: Encouraging, instructing, or romanticizing suicide or self-injury.
  • Normalization: Portraying hopelessness as aspirational or discouraging others from seeking help.
Dangerous Content

We prohibit the depiction or promotion of activities that risk severe physical harm, this includes the following:

  • Dangerous Stunts: Promoting physical challenges or "dares" that risk extreme injury or death.
  • Instructional Harm: Providing instructions on how to create weapons, explosives, or perform life-threatening acts.

Civility & Conduct

Harassment & Discrimination

We expect all users to treat one another with respect and dignity, this includes the following:

  • Targeted Abuse: Stalking, trolling, extortion, or campaigns of humiliation.
  • Hate Speech: Discrimination or dehumanization based on race, religion, orientation, gender, or disability.
  • Impersonation: Posing as others to damage their reputation or mislead the community.
Sexual Content

We maintain a professional environment and prohibit explicit content, this includes the following:

  • Explicit Acts: Nudity, sexual acts, or sexually suggestive avatar clothing and emotes.
  • Dating: Pursuing romantic relationships or engaging in unwanted flirtatious behavior.
Profanity

While Boiby allows limited profanity for users aged 13 and older, the following are prohibited:

  • Severe Profanity: Excessive swearing or using profanity to harass others.
  • Filter Bypassing: Attempting to circumvent text filters to display prohibited words.
  • Underage Exposure: Using profanity in spaces or experiences designated for all ages.
Disruptive Audio

Audio should not be used to degrade the experience of others, this includes the following:

  • Sound Spaming: Playing loud, high-pitched, or repetitive noises to disrupt conversation.
  • Abusive Audio: Uploading or playing audio containing hate speech or harassment.

Integrity & Security

Privacy

We are committed to protecting the personal data of our users, this includes the following:

  • PII Disclosure: Sharing personal information like home addresses, full legal names, or phone numbers.
  • Account Security: Attempting to solicit or share passwords and login credentials.
  • Harassment via Data: Using private information to intimidate or "dox" other members of the community.
Illegal & Regulated Goods

We prohibit the use of our platform to facilitate illegal transactions, this includes the following:

  • Regulated Goods: The sale or promotion of tobacco, alcohol, or pharmaceutical drugs.
  • Illegal Substances: Discussing the sale or manufacture of illegal narcotics.
Economy & Scams

We protect the integrity of the platform economy and user accounts, this includes the following:

  • Third-Party Trading: Buying or selling platform currency through unauthorized services.
  • Deception: Using exploits for unfair advantages or phishing for account access.
  • Spam: Repetitive messaging or creating experiences solely to drive users off-platform.
Misusing Boiby System

We prohibit the subversion of platform security and technical systems, this includes the following:

  • System Bypassing: Using VPNs to mask location for unauthorized access.
  • Evading Enforcement Actions: Creating or using an account to avoid an enforcement action taken against another account.
  • Disruptive Tools: Using automated tools or bots for disruptive tasks, or uploading obfuscated scripts.
