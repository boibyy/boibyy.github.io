Plan My Visit

Amenities: Accessible restrooms and facilities are available on Lower Ground (LG), Ground (G), and Level 2.

Lift Access: Lift access from LG-2 is located in the middle of the centre near Rocca, and from G-2 near the main entrance near South End and Onar.

Opening Hours: Tuesday - Sunday: 9AM - 9PM, Monday: 9AM - 6PM.

Development

Lichwarmer Market

Opening November 12, the Lower Ground level unveils Lichwarmer Market, a premium food hall experience anchored by Lichwarmer Supermarket. Explore a collection of refined casual dining options within our new market.

A new era begins

On November 16, Lichwarmer Square begins a new chapter. We are delighted to welcome you to a brand new destination with new retailers across Ground and Level 2. With a spacious interior and natural tones, soaring skylit atria, and ambient lighting, Lichwarmer Square has been designed to delight. Alongside much-loved local retailers, you will also discover exciting new arrivals.

Our vision is to create a modern, iconic shopping experience for Lichwarmer; blending luxury retail, fresh dining, and more, all under one roof.

And this is just the beginning; our luxury precinct on Level 1 opens next year in 2026.
