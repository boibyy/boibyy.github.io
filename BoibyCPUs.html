Requirements and Guidelines for Manufacturers

  • Memory: 16 GB LPDDR5X (LPCAMM2 advised, but can be soldered for thin-and-light laptops). 32 GB or more strongly recommended for higher-end devices. Minimum of LPDDR5X-7466. Maximum speed possible for SoC variant is recommended. Up to 128 GB supported. Dual LPCAMM2 modules are not supported.
  • Minimum Storage: PCIe Gen4 SSD, 256 GB, must not be soldered or proprietary
  • Recommended Storage: PCIe Gen4 SSD, 512 GB or higher, must not be soldered or proprietary. 1 TB or more recommended for higher-end devices.
  • Laptop Display: 1920x1080 IPS panel or higher. An 2560x1600 or higher IPS or OLED panel is recommended for higher-end devices. 16:10 or 3:2 aspect ratio advised, including resolutions such as 1920x1200, 2880x1800 (16:10), and 1920x1280, 2506x1504, 2880x1920 (3:2). 120Hz refresh rate or higher recommended for upper mid-range or higher-end devices, with support for variable refresh rate (VRR). A minimum of 400 nits of maximum brightness is required. Display must support full sRGB colour gamnut. Support for full DCI-P3 colour gamnut and HDR recommended for higher-end devices.
  • Cooling: Active cooling required (fans and heatsink, vapor chamber, or equivalent). Must be capable of maintaining stable temperatures under load without nearing throttling (105 C), and must not exceed 75 C under general usage. The SoC cannot be throttled to maintain consistent performance. Liquid metal cannot be used.
  • Minimum Wireless: Must support WiFi 6E (802.11ax, 6GHz band required) and Bluetooth 5.2 or higher. For higher-end devices, WiFi 7 and Bluetooth 5.4 LE is recommended.
  • USB Requirements: At least 2 USB Type-C ports, each supporting minimum USB 3.2 Gen 2 (10 Gbps) or higher. At least one USB4 port recommended for mid-range and higher-end devices.
  • Branding & Disclosure: Must follow all branding/advertising guidelines and disclose exact SoC model to the consumer.

License Compliance

System integrators must agree to all license terms, which may be modified or updated. Compliance is mandatory for all devices using Boiby SoCs.

Operating Systems

Officially supported operating systems are Linux and Windows. Devices must fully support both platforms.

Drivers

All drivers must be open-source and fully available. Any manufacturer-supplied drivers must fully support both Linux and Windows.

UEFI Interface

The UEFI interface may be customized for background color, model information, branding, and device-specific functions/controls. Features cannot be locked down from default stock UEFI except for CPU and RAM overclocking settings.

Design Approval

All device designs using Boiby SoCs must be approved by Boiby before being revealed or sold to consumers.

General Information

These SoCs are designed for laptops and mini PCs. System integrators should ensure proper thermal design, power delivery, wireless performance, and compliance with all manufacturer licensing and driver requirements. Boiby SoCs support a wide range of displays, memory configurations, and storage solutions, enabling flexible design for various consumer and commercial devices.
