Boiby X710 SSD

Introduction

The Boiby X710 SSD is engineered for performance, reliability, and next‑generation efficiency. Built with a custom Boiby SSD controller and advanced 236‑layer TLC NAND, it delivers exceptional speeds whether you're using the PCIe Gen 4x4 or PCIe Gen 5x2 interface.

Designed for power users, gamers, and creators, the Boiby X710 SSD provides cutting‑edge performance while maintaining thermals, efficiency, and endurance.

Performance

The X710 is the fastest DRAM-less consumer SSD with lightning‑fast read/write speeds and highly optimised firmware.

Sequential Read

Up to 7450 MB/s*

Sequential Write

Up to 6950 MB/s*

Interface Options

PCIe Gen 4x4 or Gen 5x2

Thanks to its custom controller, the X710 maintains high sustained speeds with minimal thermal drop‑off, even under heavy load.

Technical Details

Developed for durability and consistency, the X710 uses industry‑leading 236‑layer TLC NAND, paired with advanced wear‑levelling and error‑correction algorithms.
